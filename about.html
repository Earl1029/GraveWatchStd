About the studio

A university project that kept going.

Gravewatch is a student-founded creative team from Caloocan City, Philippines. We started in 2025 as a group of students at the University of Caloocan City, and we are growing toward an independent entertainment studio.

  1. Student project
  2. Creative team
  3. Developing studio
  4. Independent entertainment studio
01

From a university project to something more

Gravewatch began in 2025 as a project for our 3rd Year Finals Exhibit at the University of Caloocan City. It was meant to be a school requirement. Along the way it turned into something the team wants to keep building after the exhibit is over.

We are still studying. That is part of the story, not a footnote to it.

06

Building something beyond games

The long-term goal is an independent entertainment studio that works across games, films, art, audio, and whatever creative mediums come next.

We care about the details: the assets, the atmosphere, the small things that make a world feel alive. We want people to enjoy the main product and the work behind it.

You dream it. We make it.
